The lords' voices were spoken by Kokoro-82M (https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)
by hexgrad, released under the Apache License, Version 2.0, and then reshaped into each
lord's voice by the game's own audio code. The model itself is not part of this game.
Some of its training audio is licensed CC BY 4.0 (SIWIS dataset).
